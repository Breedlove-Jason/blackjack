How to play

Deal me in starts a hand. Choose Hit for another card, or Stand to keep your total and let the dealer play. At 21, the dealer plays automatically. Choose Next hand after the result.

  • Get closer to 21 than the dealer, without going over.
  • Aces count as 1 or 11. Face cards count as 10.
  • The dealer stands on all 17s. Equal totals are a push.
  • Two-card blackjack wins immediately unless both have it.

Fresh 52-card deck each hand. No splits or doubling. Just cards, no money.
